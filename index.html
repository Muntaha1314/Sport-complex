Management System

Football, Swimming & Tennis reservations, membership packages, wallet payments, equipment tracking, and medical report management — all in one place.

Get Started
🪪

Membership Packages

Flexible subscription plans: 2 days/week, 3 days/week, or full daily access with a fixed time slot. Each package is priced separately.

💳

Payment Tracking

Every subscription generates a payment record. Monitor paid, pending, and renewal payments per member from the manager dashboard.

🩺

Medical Report Validity

Members must hold a valid doctor-issued fitness certificate. Reports expire after 1 year. Expired reports block new subscription activation.

🔧

Equipment Maintenance

Track all gym equipment with full maintenance and repair histories. Maintenance is logged in-gym; repairs are sent to external service centers.

📊

Budget Planning

The manager dashboard shows total income from subscriptions, total costs from maintenance and repairs, and the overall net balance at a glance.

🛡️

Manager Oversight

System managers can view all member records, filter by medical status or subscription state, and query cost summaries for any period.
